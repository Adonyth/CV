## Deep-sky objects

The famous nebulae, clusters and galaxies (Pillars of Creation, Orion, Rosette, Andromeda,
the Milky Way band, etc.) are **procedurally modelled as static 3-D point-clouds** — no
third-party imagery is used for them.
