OmyteaOmytea

Founded in 2026, Omytea is the umbrella for my products and research, built as one architecture. Its product is a network of personal agents: each keeps a revisable state of its owner and acts only within the permissions its owner sets. This is the main effort, a research prototype not yet open to the public. Around it run three research programmes. Predictive world models: forecasts frozen before the fact and scored after, open to whichever methods suit a task, including an entry in ECMWF's AI Weather Quest. Embodied intelligence: devices that bring observations in and authorized actions out, carrying the working habits of an automated lab into everyday life, at the concept stage. Quantum information for world modeling: density-matrix belief states and the long question of a unified model, with AI for science as the north star. Results, negative ones included, stay on the record.
