  • Omytea — A Density-Matrix Belief-State World ModelOmytea —— 密度矩阵信念态世界模型

    Active Independent theoretical / computational physics research program · 2026独立的理论 / 计算物理研究纲领 · 2026

    A research program, in the mold of a small lab, that represents uncertainty over multiple futures as an open-system density matrix (ρ), evolves it with an operator/channel algebra, and revises it through a proper-scored, auditable measurement-update ledger — aimed ultimately at a Grand Unified World Model. The honest current headline is a calibrated, auditable system with no demonstrated raw-accuracy advantage over strong classical baselines; the program makes no primacy or "beats-baseline" claim. In-preparation manuscripts include an honest negative-result systems paper (real-data L3 calibration/coverage certificate) and a Lindblad-form open-system extension of Cui & Ma (2026).一个以小型实验室方式运作的研究纲领，用开系统密度矩阵（ρ）表示多重未来的不确定性，由算子/信道代数演化，并通过可审计、经正当评分的测量更新账本进行修正，终极目标指向大统一世界模型。诚实的当前结论是：得到一个可校准、可审计的系统，但在原始精度上未证明胜过强经典基线；纲领不作任何首创或"胜过基线"主张。撰写中的手稿包括一篇诚实的负结果系统论文（真实数据 L3 校准/覆盖率证书）与对 Cui & Ma（2026）的 Lindblad 开系统扩展。
